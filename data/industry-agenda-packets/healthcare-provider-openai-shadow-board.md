# Healthcare Provider Network - Shadow Board Packet

## Decision Prompt
Should the board approve a phased rollout of OpenAI-powered copilots for clinical documentation, patient communications, and access-center triage?

## Agenda
1. Safety and quality governance
2. Privacy and cyber resilience
3. Workforce adoption and human-in-the-loop design
4. ROI and measurable outcomes

## Board Inputs
- Proposed pilot in two hospital regions and one ambulatory network.
- Human review required for all patient-facing outputs in phase 1.
- Target outcomes: reduced documentation burden, improved portal response times, lower access-center wait times.

## Challenge Questions
- How do we verify safe model behavior across high-variance patient scenarios?
- What control points are required to avoid privacy and security regressions?
- Which clinical roles gain capacity, and where could workload shift negatively?
- What stop/go criteria trigger expansion beyond pilot scope?
