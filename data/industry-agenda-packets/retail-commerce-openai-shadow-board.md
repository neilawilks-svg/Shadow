# Retail and E-Commerce - Shadow Board Packet

## Decision Prompt
Should the board launch OpenAI-powered merchandising, customer-service, and pricing copilots before the next peak commerce cycle?

## Agenda
1. Customer experience impact and trust
2. Payment and data security guardrails
3. Margin and productivity outcomes
4. Channel operations and workforce readiness

## Board Inputs
- Planned use cases: product-copy generation, service-agent assist, personalized merchandising support.
- Sensitive transactions and payment actions remain system-constrained in phase 1.
- Success goals: increase conversion, reduce handle time, improve catalog velocity.

## Challenge Questions
- How do we improve conversion without creating trust or brand risk?
- What controls ensure payment-card and consumer data protections remain robust?
- Where does automation produce true margin gains versus hidden operational cost?
- What adoption playbook is needed for store, digital, and support teams?
