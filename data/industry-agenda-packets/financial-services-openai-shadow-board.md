# Financial Services Institution - Shadow Board Packet

## Decision Prompt
Should the board authorize OpenAI-based agents for client servicing, internal operations, and compliance acceleration in a controlled production program?

## Agenda
1. Model governance and control architecture
2. Cyber risk and incident readiness
3. Fraud and operational resilience implications
4. Revenue, cost, and supervisory confidence

## Board Inputs
- Initial scope includes contact-center summarization, policy Q&A, and operations workflow copilots.
- Regulated decisions remain human-approved in phase 1.
- Pilot objective: improve service quality while reducing cycle times in middle-office operations.

## Challenge Questions
- Which use cases require the strictest model risk controls?
- How do we evidence governance quality to regulators and auditors?
- What failure modes create customer harm or disclosure obligations?
- What controls preserve upside while containing operational risk?
