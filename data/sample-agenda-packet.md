# Sample Agenda Packet

## Meeting Theme
Evaluate launch readiness for a virtual board advisory offering.

## Agenda Topics
1. Target market and differentiation
2. Governance model and risk controls
3. Delivery operating model and talent plan
4. Commercial model and ROI timeline

## Prompting Context
- Client mix includes public-sector and regulated industries.
- 90-day pilot required with clear success metrics.
- Board concern: balancing speed with trust and compliance.
